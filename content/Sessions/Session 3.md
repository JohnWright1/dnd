# Recap

[[Aukan]], [[Avery Wrendale]], and [[Paeola Diggledoo]] found themselves in the overseer's office with [[Beatrice Flowerheart]] the overseer of the [[Gnomegarde Central Mine]] who thanked them for their assistance with the ogre in the lower mines and for sealing the entrance, she paid them for the services and seemed unaware of any issues that they had noticed since they were last in the office. 

She had no recollection of a [[Dagrun Kragdigger]] who was the previous overseer, as the group were leaving the young [[Perri Leafblade]] spoke up to the party mentioning that he knew [[Dagrun Kragdigger]] but couldn't remember everything as if the details were spotty and hazy.

The party noticed that [[Perri Leafblade]] had a similar marking on his arm of a crescent moon similar to the one that each of them had but significantly less pronounced that theirs. He explained how he was born under a lesser dragon moon and his mom always talked about how he was special and that is what caused him to have this birthmark but he mentioned that it wasn't always visible.

The party decided to head over to try find [[Adzak]] to give him his share of the reward for assisting with the quest and to tell him off all the changes at the mines.

[[Adzak]] made his way into the town of [[Tinkerville]], where he came across [[Sturdy Supplies]] the main general store in the town, it was ran by a Jovial dwarf who went by the name Porky. [[Adzak]] showed great resistance being able to control his natural bloodlust and rage towards the store owner caused by the his previous overseer and slave enforcer.

[[Adzak]] was able to trade the gems he'd gathered in exchange for enough gold to purchase some starter equipment, he decided to head to the tavern to get a room and await the rest of the party to see if they kept their end of the bargain.

The rest of the party made their way into the town and hit up the shop to re-supply on some supplies and inquire if anyone had seen an Orc pass through town, they were able to discover that he had headed into the local tavern for a place to stay.

The party using [[Paeola Diggledoo]]'s familiar were able to meet up and discuss what had happened, at first there was shock and disbelief that what they were saying could be true and that things had really changed as they have said. The party now together decided to spend the night at the inn.

In the middle of the night [[Paeola Diggledoo]] who slept in the main inn was visited by a pack of Ghouls who roamed around the inn, she kept an eye on the monsters who then later left as the sun arose deciding not to enter or attack the inn, while this was happening [[Paeola Diggledoo]] could feel the power radiating from the [[Black Transcendent Crystal]] that the party was carrying.

As the party headed back towards [[Gnomegarde]] they happened upon [[Tinkerville Cemetery]] where they had an encounter with a Wight that after defeated resulted in them finding a bag of holding.

This is where we left off. Where do the adventures want to go next? Any leads to follow? Personal Interests?
