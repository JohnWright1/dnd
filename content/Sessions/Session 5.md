# Recap

The party awakens to find themselves in a jail of sorts, each in separate cells. As they come round they slowly begin to recall how they ended up in this situation.

The events of the previous day begin to come back to them.

The party recall entering the Gemhold Academy searching for answers about the mysterious crystal they were carrying.

As soon as they approached the reception they were suddenly surrounded by a group of crystal golems who were seemingly threatened by the crystal they were carrying. The group quickly became isolated under a bubble of sorts that prevented their escape or movement.

The party noticed a collection of people making their way down the staircase to check on the commotion as a gas seeped into the bubble, before they knew it they were losing consciousness. 

When the party awoke they found themselves in a temple fighting off a group of snake-people that none of the party have ever encountered or heard of before. 

To the surprise of the party they were not alone at the temple and instead met a young human rogue called Priscilla Jackknife, she explained how she had found herself in a spot of trouble and ended up inside the prison cell and saw them arriving unconscious, she was awoken a shadowy figure had entered the cell with Aukan and placed the ring on his finger before disappearing into thin air. Suddenly a bright light filled the room and then she found herself here alongside all of you.

Throughout the temple they noticed language and architecture similar to that of Zepharyon Empire who had disappeared from Erodar over a thousand years ago back in the time of magic.

After defeating the final assailant the party in the temple found themselves back in the prison cell feeling stronger and more powerful, a bright white light was glowing from Aukan's hand before fading where the ring was now permanently tattooed into his skin.
