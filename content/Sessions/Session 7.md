# Recap

XP Gain:  300

In our last session...

In the middle of the night our party received some unexpected visitors, Adzak the barbarian awoke in the middle of the night to the sound of someone sneaking about out side of the room he was resting in.

As he rushed to the door he noticed a envelope had been pushed through a small gap under the doorway, the envelope contained two letters. 

The back of the envelope had an insignia - a chalice full of fire embossed onto the back.

Unable to read, Adzak ignored the two letters, and noticed as his hand touched the embossed insignia that the insignia seemed to set on fire and burn off the back of the letter, leaving the envelope blank.

In the morning Adzak headed down to greet the rest of the party who were enjoying their breakfast. He told them of what happened during the night prior and gave them the letters to see if they'd have any more luck understanding them than he would.

The party was able to gather there were there were two ciphers in the letters the first revealed that at the [[Gearglass Skyport]] at sunset there would be a secret revealed in the adjacent alleyway, that will help them find the shipment they have been looking for.

The second cipher lead to a riddle.

> [!NOTE] Cipher
> A castle made of brick and stone
Takes twenty years to make
The bricks I need to finish it
How many does it take? 
-- One Brick

After solving the riddle they discovered that they would need to use a brick to help them at some point.

While heading towards the [[Gearglass Skyport]] the party do some shopping and get a lay of the area.

While travelling the party come across several leaflets and flyers for the ultimate test of might and strength with a group fighting competition occurring in the city of [[Windrock]], that promises combatants honor, glory and rewards beyond their wildest dreams.

The party made it to the [[Gearglass Skyport]], entering the alley between the Skyport and a flower shop, as the sun fell they noticed a gap in the wall where they could insert a brick into the wall. 

As they pushed the brick into the wall suddenly on the wall near them further down into the alleyway, an glimmering began to appear as a entrance suddenly materialized through it the party could see into a storage room deep inside of the Skyport.

The party headed into the room, the room was full of documents and crates, on a shelf the party noticed a crate with the the crest of the [[Gemhold]] Academy.

The group can found a contract of employment for the thieves that says:

> [!NOTE] Letter
> "Everything has been arranged. There will be no resistance, enter the Gemhold academies storage center and take the large crate on the east of the room. Deliver it to the Gearglass Skyport for midnight where you will be met by our contacts - 250 Gold."

 At the back of the letter you they found a symbol of a dented coffin with a skeletal hand reaching out.

While in the storage facility the party encountered the group of thieves who were waiting for their midnight drop off. The party quickly dispatched of the thieves. 

While cleaning up and looting the thieves the party suddenly heard commotion behind them as another group had entered the storage facility behind them. The group wore long white robes with a chalice of fire on each side of the robe. 

The group thanked the party for their assistance in the matter and notified the party that they've moved the crystal into a different crate for them to take back to the academy. Clearly outnumbered and unsure of the situation the party decided to be cautious. 

While the party were leaving they noticed that several members of the group began changing or shape shifting into the thieves that the party had dispatched of.

The party headed back to the tavern to finish their day, before heading back to the Gemhold academy.

In the middle of the night the party was awoke, they found out that there was an explosion north of the city as a cargo airship had exploded en-route to [[Windrock]].
