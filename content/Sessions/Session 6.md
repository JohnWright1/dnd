# Recap
The last time the party were together they were in Gemhold Prison where they were mistakenly imprisoned for being in possession of a black transcendent crystal. The party were interrogated over a few hours until it was truthfully revealed that the party found the crystal embedded inside the body of an Ogre that was being empowered by the crystal.

It was revealed to the party how [[Transcendent Crystals]] works and how the [[Black Transcendent Crystal]] is a man-made crystal imbued with necrotic power, they asked if the party were to learn anymore about the crystal or the person who created it to let the group know.

While they were at the Prison they met with [[Croakus Fenweave]]. He informed the group of a stolen shipment of [[Transcendent Crystals]] from the illustrious [[Gemhold]] Academy, you have been requested to assist with getting back the stolen shipment.

Croakus is suspicious of how it was possible for the crystals to have been stolen from the Academy with it's impressive security, he believes that their might be people on the inside who have assisted or a greater organisation is involved, he is interested in the group finding out any more information they can while getting the shipment back.

He'll pay handsomely for the return of the crystals, and will be happy to pay for additional information that is discovered about who hired the group to steal the shipment.

Previously [[Priscilla Jackknife]] had ended up having some discussions with some local ruffians in the [[Copper Kettle Tavern]], she was able to get some information about how the shipment is going to be taken to [[Gearglass Skyport]] tomorrow night to be handed over to another faction that the shipment has been sold to.

The party headed to bed for the night to get out searching for more information in the morning.
