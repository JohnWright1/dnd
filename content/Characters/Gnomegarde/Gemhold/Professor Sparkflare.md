# Appearance

Professor Sparkflare is a master of defensive enchantments, renowned for his innovative techniques and practical applications in the field. He is a lively and energetic half-elf, with a shock of bright red hair that seems to have a life of its own, often sticking out in wild directions. Her eyes, a vivid green, sparkle with an infectious enthusiasm and a hint of mischief.

He favors practical clothing that allows for ease of movement, usually seen in sturdy leather aprons over simple yet colorful tunics. His hands bear the marks of countless experiments, with faint scorch marks and tiny nicks testifying to his hands-on approach to teaching.

Professor Sparkflare’s classroom is a hub of activity, filled with the sounds of enchantments being cast and tools being wielded. He encourages his students to experiment and innovate, often joining in their practical exercises with boundless energy. His teaching style is dynamic and engaging, making even the most complex defensive spells feel accessible and exciting.

### **Professor Sparkflare**  
*Medium humanoid (elf), Lawful Good*  
**Armor Class:** 14 (Mage Armor)  
**Hit Points:** 65 (10d6 + 20)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.  

**STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA**  
10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4)  

**Saving Throws:** Constitution +6, Charisma +8  
**Skills:** Arcana +7, History +7, Insight +5, Persuasion +8  
**Damage Resistances:** Radiant  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common, Elvish, Draconic  

**Elf Traits:**  
- *Fey Ancestry*: Advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and cannot be put to sleep by magic.  
- *Trance*: Does not need to sleep, instead meditating for 4 hours.

### **Sorcery Features**  

**Sorcery Points:** 10 (recharges on long rest)  
**Metamagic:**  
- *Heightened Spell*: Impose disadvantage on a target’s saving throw.  
- *Quickened Spell*: Cast a spell as a bonus action.  

**Font of Magic:** Professor Sparkflare can convert sorcery points into spell slots or vice versa.

### **Actions**  

**Quarterstaff (melee):** +2 to hit, 1d6 bludgeoning damage (or 1d8 if wielded two-handed).  

**Radiant Surge (Recharge 6):** Professor Sparkflare channels her innate connection to radiant energy, releasing a burst of light. Each creature within 20 feet must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d10 radiant damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.

### **Personality and Background**  
Professor Sparkflare is a renowned scholar of magical theory, specializing in the study of dimensional energies and ancient artifacts. Her lawful good alignment reflects her unwavering commitment to using her knowledge and power for the betterment of society. She is a mentor to many aspiring sorcerers and adventurers, though her strict adherence to rules and ethics can sometimes make her seem stern.
