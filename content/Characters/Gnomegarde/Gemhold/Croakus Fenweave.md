# Appearance & Description

Croakus Fenweave commands attention with an aura as deep and mysterious as the marshlands he calls home. His appearance is both regal and arcane, a testament to his mastery of Ribbari sorcery.

Croakus stands slightly taller than most Ribbari, his posture straighter and more dignified. His skin is a rich, mossy green with subtle golden flecks that shimmer like sunlight on a still pond, hinting at the magical energy coursing through him. Webbed hands are dexterous and expressive, with fingers that seem to dance in the air as he casts his intricate spells. His eyes are luminous orbs of amber, glowing softly in the dark—a window to the wisdom and power he carries.

He dons a robe woven from enchanted swamp reeds and embroidered with runic symbols that pulse faintly with light. The robe’s flowing edges ripple like water, as if responding to his connection to dimensional energies. Around his neck hangs a pendant shaped like a shard of a Transcendent Crystal, imbued with a fragment of essence from a benevolent Rift. The pendant glows faintly, and some claim it guides his visions and prophecies.

Croakus’s staff, carved from the ancient Driftwood Trees of the Fenlands, is gnarled yet elegant. Its tip holds a clear gem swirling with iridescent mist—the mark of his ability to manipulate dimensional forces. The staff’s surface is etched with intricate depictions of his people's myths, its base rooted in the soil of ages.

Despite his mystic grandeur, Croakus carries the subtle marks of his amphibian nature: a soft croak beneath his breath when he’s lost in thought, the faint scent of fresh rain that lingers around him, and an effortless grace in water. His presence is deeply calming yet endlessly enigmatic, embodying the essence of the Ribbari and the untold mysteries of the Rifts.

# Statblock

### **Croakus Fenweave**  
*Medium humanoid (Ribbari), Lawful Good
**Armor Class:** 13 (Mage Armor)  
**Hit Points:** 45 (7d6 + 14)  
**Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| **STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA** |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 8 (-1)  | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws:** Constitution +5, Charisma +7  
**Skills:** Arcana +4, Insight +5, Nature +4, Persuasion +7  
**Damage Resistances:** Poison  
**Condition Immunities:** Poisoned  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12  
**Languages:** Common, Aquan, Sylvan  

**Amphibious:** Croakus can breathe air and water.  

**Standing Leap:** Croakus's long jump is up to 20 feet, and his high jump is up to 10 feet, with or without a running start.

---

### **Sorcery Features**

**Sorcery Points:** 7 (recharges on long rest)  
**Metamagic:**  
- *Twin Spell*: Target a second creature with a spell that normally targets only one.  
- *Careful Spell*: Allow up to 4 creatures to automatically succeed on their saving throws against his spells.  

**Rift Connection:** When Croakus absorbs a Rift into his Transcendent Crystal, he gains temporary access to a spell or ability from that Rift’s dimension (DM’s discretion). This feature is unique to him as a Ribbari Riftcaster.

---

### **Spellcasting**  
**Spellcasting Ability:** Charisma (Spell Save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks)  
**Cantrips (at will):** *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*, *Frostbite*, *Minor Illusion*, *Shape Water*  
**1st Level (4 slots):** *Mage Armor*, *Shield*, *Charm Person*  
**2nd Level (3 slots):** *Misty Step*, *Hold Person*  
**3rd Level (3 slots):** *Counterspell*, *Fireball*, *Water Breathing*  
**4th Level (1 slot):** *Dimension Door*  

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack (Optional):** Croakus can make two melee attacks with his staff if engaged in close combat.

**Quarterstaff (melee):** +2 to hit, 1d6-1 bludgeoning damage (or 1d8-1 if wielded two-handed).  

**Rift Surge (Recharge 6):** Croakus channels his crystal’s absorbed energy, releasing a burst of dimensional power. Each creature within 20 feet must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d10 force damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.

---

### **Equipment**  
- **Transcendent Crystal Pendant**: Absorbs Rift energy and allows him to channel limited dimensional magic.  
- **Robe of the Swamplord**: Grants him resistance to poison and advantage on saving throws against being restrained.  
- **Fenwood Staff**: Arcane focus that doubles as a quarterstaff.
