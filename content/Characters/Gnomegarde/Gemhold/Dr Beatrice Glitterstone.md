# Appearance

Dr. Glitterstone is an esteemed figure in the world of crystal magic, known for her pioneering work on crystal resonance. She is a stately gnome with a warm yet commanding presence. Her hair, a silvery cascade that glitters like the crystals she studies, is often kept in an elegant bun adorned with crystal pins. Her sharp, intelligent eyes are a deep shade of blue, reflecting her vast knowledge and curiosity.

Dr. Glitterstone’s attire is as practical as it is stylish, often seen wearing finely tailored robes in shades of deep purple and midnight blue, embroidered with silver thread that mimics the patterns of crystalline structures. Around her neck, she wears a pendant that holds a rare, luminescent crystal—an artifact from one of her most significant discoveries. 

Her lectures are famed for their clarity and depth, often supplemented with live demonstrations that leave her students in awe. She has a knack for making complex concepts accessible, inspiring her students with both her brilliance and her approachable demeanor.
