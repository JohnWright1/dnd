# Appearance

Gloria is a striking figure, a blend of elegance and unassuming warmth. With a keen eye for beauty, she is always impeccably dressed, often in flowing gowns of rich, jewel-toned fabrics that reflect her love for gemstones. Her silver hair, usually pinned up in an elaborate style, adds an air of timeless sophistication, while her sparkling green eyes reveal a sharp intellect and a passion for her craft.

Her demeanor is gracious and welcoming, making every visitor to her shop feel like a cherished guest. Gloria’s voice carries a melodic quality, soothing and confident, as she shares her extensive knowledge of gems and crystals with customers. Her fingers, adorned with an array of delicate rings, move gracefully as she handles each stone with the utmost care and reverence.

Despite her refined appearance, Gloria is not afraid to roll up her sleeves and delve into the more laborious aspects of her business. Her dedication to her shop and her customers is evident in every aspect of her work, from the meticulous organization of her displays to the thoughtful way she engages with each person who walks through her door.

Gloria's warm personality and deep knowledge of her trade make her a beloved figure in the lower city, and her shop, "Gloria's Glorious Gems," is a testament to her lifelong dedication to the art of gemology.
