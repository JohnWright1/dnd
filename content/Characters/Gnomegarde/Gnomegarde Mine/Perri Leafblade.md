# Appearance & Description

Perri Leafblade is a youthful, vibrant gnome, full of energy and enthusiasm. He stands a bit shorter than the average gnome, with a lean, nimble build that allows him to dart around the mine overseer's office with ease. His bright green hair is unruly, often tied back in a short ponytail to keep it out of his sparkling blue eyes, which are always wide with curiosity and excitement.

He sports a practical yet slightly over-sized tunic, cinched at the waist with a leather belt from which hang various pouches and tools needed for his daily tasks. His attire is completed with sturdy brown boots, scuffed from frequent movement but still holding up well. A pair of round spectacles often perch on his nose, slightly askew from his constant adjusting.

Perri's hands are always busy, either scribbling away on parchment or organizing stacks of documents. Despite his youthful appearance, he has an air of determination and diligence about him, always eager to impress his superiors and prove his worth. His demeanor is friendly and approachable, often seen chatting animatedly with his colleagues, his high-pitched voice filled with enthusiasm.

Overall, Perri Leafblade’s appearance reflects his hardworking nature, his vibrant personality, and his eagerness to learn and grow within the overseer's office.
