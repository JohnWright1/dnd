# Appearance & Description

Thorne Greysteel is a seasoned human ranger in his early 40s, known throughout the realm for his stoic demeanor, unmatched tracking skills, and deep connection to the wild. As the leader of the Adventurers' Guild in **Gnomegarde**, Thorne serves as both protector and mentor to a diverse band of adventurers.

Thorne learned survival from his hermit grandfather, a former scout of the royal army. After years of wandering the untamed frontiers, he earned a reputation for rescuing lost travelers, slaying marauding beasts, and uncovering forgotten ruins. His exploits eventually led him to Gnomegarde, where his calm leadership and tactical brilliance earned him the guildmaster’s mantle.

Thorne’s closest companion is **Branwyn**, a silver-furred wolf he rescued as a pup from a collapsing cave. The two share an almost telepathic bond—Branwyn scouts ahead, senses danger, and guards the guildhall with unwavering loyalty.

Despite his grizzled exterior, Thorne is deeply respected by the gnomes for his patience and fairness. He’s known to sit quietly in the guild’s tavern, sipping herbal tea while listening to young adventurers recount their quests. His advice is always brief, but profound.
