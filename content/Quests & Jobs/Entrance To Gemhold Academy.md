# Details

Entrance will be provided if the party immediately brings the crystal to a guard who may be able to assist or shows it an appropriate person (possibly the party will be arrested if they can't convince the guards of the academy that they found the crystal.)

If the party attempts to enter the grounds of the Gemhold without reporting the crystal that they had found several magical golems will approach the party and attempt to arrest or detain them for holding an illegal magical item.

If they are captured they will be interrogated by Dr Glitterstone who will inquire into why the party has the magical item and who gave it to them, the golems/staff will use an item to verify that Paeola has the capabilities to use magic and could have caused the creation of the item.

If the party are there to get rid of the gem they will be asked if they wish to attend the destruction of the object as they may find it interesting to see the process.
