# Questgiver

- Lavender - Adventurers Guild
# Details

In the outskirts of Gnomegarde there have been issues with attacks on travelling trade caravans by Goblins, the guild is interested in the assistance of any adventurers who are able to protect the caravans and get to the root of the cause.

Goblins are known to live in forest and mountains surrounding the roads but it is rare for them to attack in these parts, so the change in behavior is most unusual.

Bonus gold will be awarded for dealing with the goblins attacking the caravan and dealing with the sudden cause of the attacks.

# Objectives

 - [ ] Protect the merchants 
 - [ ] Defend the caravan from harm
 - [ ] Defeat the goblins
 - [ ] Find the cause of the goblin attacks
 - [ ] Deal with cause of the goblin change in attitude
