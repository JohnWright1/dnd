# Objectives

The party awakens in the holding cells of the Gemhold Academy, they quickly notice that all their possessions have been taken from them, including their weapons and any items they were carrying.

After a short time a guard approaches and begins to take the party members one-by-one for questioning. 

Each of the party members are taken into a small room, that has a circle carved into the ground with 5 crystals placed at equal distances around the outside. In the corner of the room is the black transcendent crystal that the party had previously in their possession.

The party will be questioned about how they came into possession of the crystal, what they know about the crystal, what they know about transcendent crystals, if they know anything about imbuing crystals with magical power and if they know anyone who can use magic.

They will be forced/asked to assist with dealing with the crystal which involves releasing the magic from the crystal and dealing with any consequences. 

It is at this point that [[Professor Sparkflare]] will explain to the party about [[The Rifts]], and how destroying the crystal may release some of the after effects from [[The Rifts]], including releasing creatures, that may not be from this world.

As a thanks for the assistance [[Professor Sparkflare]] will give the party a crystal that will allow the party to communicate more easily from greater distances and allow her to get back in contact with the party with any information she can gather with regards to the mysterious figure and the crystal they encountered. 

She will ask the party to take Priscilla with them and order that she stays with them as part of her bail sentence, hoping that the good deeds she suspects the party might do will rub off on her and change her for the better.

She recommends that if the group want to find themselves in good standing that the city would really appreciate their assistance in dealing with a local smuggling ring who had recently stolen a shipment of [[Transcendent Crystals]] from the academy.

There are rumors that the group plan's to unleash a Rift within the city, she recommends that the group head to the [[Copper Kettle Tavern]] to see if they can find any information or the rumored fence that is in the lower city - [[No Question's Asked]]
