# Description

Tinkerville Cemetery, nestled on the outskirts of the quaint village of Tinkerville, is an unassuming yet slightly eerie place. The cemetery is modest in size, bordered by a iron fence that has seen better days, its paint peeling and some sections overtaken by creeping ivy. A narrow, cobblestone path winds through the graves, leading to a small, weathered chapel that stands as the centerpiece of the cemetery.

By day, the cemetery appears peaceful and somewhat neglected, with headstones varying in age and craftsmanship dotting the overgrown grass. Ancient oaks and willows cast long shadows, their branches swaying gently in the breeze. Wildflowers and unkempt bushes add a touch of color to the otherwise somber setting, and the occasional bird song or rustle of leaves are the only sounds that break the silence.

However, as dusk falls, an eerie transformation occurs. The once tranquil atmosphere becomes charged with an unsettling energy.

The chapel interior, dimly lit by flickering candles, is filled with arcane symbols and enchanted objects. Shelves lined with dusty tomes and peculiar artifacts hint at the dark studies that occupy the necromancer's time. In the heart of the chapel, a large, ornate altar serves as the focal point for sinister rituals.

The necromancer, a shadowy figure draped in tattered robes, moves silently among the graves, tending to their dark arts. The air grows colder and the shadows seem to deepen, as if the cemetery itself is under the necromancer's spell. It's said that the spirits of the deceased are restless here, drawn to the necromancer's power and bound to their will.

Tinkerville Cemetery, while seemingly ordinary by day, harbors a dark and mysterious secret, making it a place of both sorrow and sinister enchantment in the quiet village.

**Bag of holding**

Describe the appearance of a bag of holding, found inside the chapel

The bag of holding found inside the chapel is an item of both mystery and allure. Made from dark, supple leather that feels cool to the touch, the bag exudes an almost otherworldly presence. Its surface is adorned with intricate runes and symbols that seem to shift and glimmer as they catch the light, hinting at the powerful enchantments contained within.

The bag is about the size of a large satchel, with a reinforced strap for easy carrying across the shoulder or back. The opening is secured by a sturdy, ornate clasp in the shape of a coiled dragon, its eyes set with tiny, glinting gemstones. Upon closer inspection, the interior of the bag reveals a void-like darkness that seems impossibly vast, an enchanted space far larger than its outer appearance suggests.

Inside, the bag feels weightless regardless of what is placed within, making it an invaluable tool for adventurers and collectors alike. The craftsmanship is both elegant and functional, blending seamlessly with the arcane nature of the chapel's hidden treasures. This bag of holding is not merely a container; it is a relic of immense utility and a testament to the ancient magics that permeate the chapel.
