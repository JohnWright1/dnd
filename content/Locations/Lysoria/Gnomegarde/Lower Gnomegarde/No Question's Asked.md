# Description

"No Questions Asked" is a dimly lit pawnshop tucked away in a narrow, shadowy alley of the lower city. The exterior is nondescript, with weathered wooden planks and a creaky sign hanging above the door, barely visible under layers of grime. A single lantern, emitting a weak, flickering light, casts eerie shadows across the entrance.

Inside, the air is thick with the scent of dust and old wood. The shop is cluttered with shelves and display cases filled with a mismatched assortment of items—everything from tarnished silverware to mysterious artifacts. The lighting is intentionally dim, creating an atmosphere of secrecy and caution. The walls are lined with faded tapestries and framed maps of dubious origin, adding to the shop's shady character.

The counter, made of dark, heavy wood, is scratched and worn from years of haggling and transactions. Behind it stands the shop's owner, a grizzled, middle-aged human with a perpetual scowl and eyes that miss nothing. He’s dressed in a long coat with deep pockets, always ready to quickly stash or retrieve an item. His demeanor is gruff, and he speaks in a low, gravelly voice, making it clear that discretion is the shop's primary offering.

Customers of all sorts—roguish types, desperate individuals, and those with secrets to keep—frequent the shop. Transactions are quick and quiet, with few words exchanged and even fewer questions asked. Crystals, gems, and other valuables change hands swiftly, disappearing into the shop's hidden recesses. This is a place where anything of value can be sold or bought, provided you're willing to keep your own secrets and pay the right price.
