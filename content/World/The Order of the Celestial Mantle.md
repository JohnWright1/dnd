The Order of the Celestial Mantle is an enigmatic group deeply entwined with the lore of the Lunarians. Cloaked in secrecy, the order's origins are as ancient as the dragons they venerate. Their headquarters is hidden within the Whispering Peaks, a mountain range perpetually shrouded in mist, accessible only by those who possess the knowledge of its hidden pathways.

Members of the Celestial Mantle are distinguished by their flowing midnight-blue robes, embroidered with silver thread in intricate patterns that mimic the constellations. They wear amulets crafted from shards of Transcendent Crystals, believed to resonate with the power of the dragon moon. Their faces are often concealed by hoods, and their identities are known only to a select few.

The order’s library, the Archive of Scales, is a vast chamber filled with ancient tomes, scrolls, and crystal tablets that document the prophecies and history of the Lunarians. Here, scholars and mystics of the Celestial Mantle tirelessly study the movements of the stars and the dragon moons, seeking to unravel the mysteries of the Lunarians and their destined role in the return of magic to Erodar.

Rituals within the order are steeped in symbolism and celestial alignment. They gather at the apex of the mountain during lunar eclipses and other celestial events, performing ancient rites and chanting incantations that have been passed down through generations. It is during these ceremonies that they believe the voices of the ancient dragons speak through the Transcendent Crystals, guiding the Lunarians in their quest.

The Celestial Mantle believes that the Lunarians, born under the blessed dragon moon, are the key to an ancient prophecy that foretells the resurgence of true magic in the world. They see themselves as the guardians of this knowledge, tasked with protecting the Lunarians and ensuring they fulfill their destiny. Their ultimate goal is to reunite the disparate fragments of magic and restore Erodar to its former glory, a world where wonder and enchantment flow freely once more.
