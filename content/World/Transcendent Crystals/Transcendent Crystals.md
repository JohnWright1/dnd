Transcendent Crystals are everywhere. They're the lifeblood of [[Erodar]], present in all monsters and magical creatures, they can be found naturally occurring in many areas of the world. 

Each crystal has unique properties that vary based on how they are acquired, naturally occurring crystals are usually clear in colour and vary in size. Crystals that are taken from magic creatures and monsters usually have a colour that relates to the type of the creature, the stronger the creature the greater and more valuable the crystal is.

These crystals are capable of harnessing simple magical abilities, from healing wounds to illuminating the dark. They are sought after and highly valued, they are purchased by merchants all over Erodar, additionally they can be used to enchant existing weapons and to craft magical items.

[[Crystal Shapers]] are able to modify these crystals to produce magical effects, each kingdom has universities dedicated to the study of these crystals, the [[Gemhold]] academy in Gnomegarde is one such place. 

There also several stores in the city that can make use of the items to craft magical items, armor and weaponry.
