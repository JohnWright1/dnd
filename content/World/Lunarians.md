There are two moons in the sky.

The smaller moon named Dionne and the larger moon Oberon.

When a dragon comes to certain ages the moons are affected.

Smaller moon is affected by a dragon becoming adult.
Larger moon is affected by a dragon becoming ancient.

When the moon is under these affects the world has different effects.

The most notable being when the a black dragon became ancient and caused two weeks of darkness, where the dead rose again for the duration...
