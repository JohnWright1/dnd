# Appearance

## Main Hall

- The main hall is made of stone and reinforced with timber beams, the hall has a vaulted ceiling strung with lanterns powered by crystals.
- The walls are covered in maps, wanted posters, and mission boards.
- The floor is covered in worn flagstones with scattered rugs—some stitched from monster hides, others woven artisans with gnomish designs.
- There is a large fireplace to one side of the room, with tables and chairs all over the main hall.
- There is a large reception in front of your as you enter with a curved staircase either side of the reception leading upstairs, at the reception you notice adventurers chatting with some of the staff and what seems to be non-adventurers dropping off requests and asking about contract prices.

## Guildmaster’s Alcove

A semi-private nook with a large desk carved from petrified wood, covered in scrolls, maps, and reports. Branwyn’s bed—a thick fur-lined mat—is beside it.

The room is decorated with trophies from past quests—fangs, feathers, and a broken crown—hang above the desk. A mural behind him depicts the founding of the guild, with Thorne at its center.
