### 🔥 The Ember Saints: Gnomegarde’s Purifying Flame

Where _The Dented Coffin_ thrives in death and decay, _The Ember Saints_ burn with righteous fury. This fanatical criminal order believes Gnomegarde is a city corrupted by necromancy, vice, and spiritual rot—and they’ve taken it upon themselves to cleanse it, one sinner at a time.

#### 🔱 Leadership: High Purifier Tyrion Fording

At the head of the Ember Saints stands _High Purifier [[Tyriel]]_, a former cleric turned extremist. Tyrion was excommunicated for his violent interpretations of scripture. Rather than repent, he built a following of zealots who see fire not just as holy, but as justice incarnate.

Tyrion wears scorched ceremonial robes and a mask shaped like a flame-wreathed skull. His sermons are equal parts prophecy and threat, and his followers believe he receives visions directly from the Flame itself. He considers Gabriel Danton’s undead existence a blasphemy that must be erased, and has declared _The Dented Coffin_ “an affront to divine order.”

#### 🔥 Structure and Operations

The Ember Saints operate like a cult with criminal efficiency. 

Their sanctum, known as _The Furnace Cathedral_, is a repurposed temple deep within Gnomegarde’s volcanic district. Its walls are blackened with soot, and its altars blaze with eternal fire. Only the faithful may enter—and only the penitent leave.

#### 🔥 Symbolism and Reputation

Their emblem is a chalice full of fire. To the public, they pose as spiritual reformers. To the underworld, they’re holy terrorists. Their presence is marked by scorched symbols and the scent of incense and ash.
