### 🕯️ The Dented Coffin: Gnomegarde’s Shadow Syndicate

In the underbelly of Gnomegarde, where the cobbled streets twist into forgotten alleys and the lanterns flicker with more than just wind, whispers of _The Dented Coffin_ echo like a funeral dirge. This criminal organization is the city's most feared syndicate—equal parts myth and menace.

#### 🧟‍♂️ Leadership: Gabriel "The Dent" Danton

At its helm is the Undead Underboss, [[Gabriel Danton]] —once a notorious enforcer in Gnomegarde’s old guard. His nickname, _The Dent_, comes from the jagged fracture in his skull where a betrayal nearly ended him. Instead, he rose from his grave, embalmed in fury and ambition, and built an empire from the bones of his enemies.

#### 🕯️ Symbolism and Reputation

The organization’s emblem is a dented coffin with a single skeletal hand reaching out. It’s etched into alley walls, carved into gravestones, and burned into the memories of those who’ve crossed them. To speak their name aloud is to invite a visit. To owe them a debt is to risk burial—alive or otherwise.
