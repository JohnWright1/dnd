### **The Wounding Vault**

The box in Priscilla’s possession, known among scholars as the *Wounding Vault*, is a relic of profound mystery and danger. Forged in the volatile chaos of a Rift, the Vault bridges dimensions in its very essence. It is both a physical container and a magical bond, an artifact of immense utility but equally devastating cost.

#### **Origins**

The Wounding Vault is said to have emerged from a Rift connected to a realm of *Binding and Sacrifice*. This dimension is governed by complex, arcane laws that exchange power for pain—a concept alien to the natural world. Artifacts from this realm are imbued with enchantments that require personal sacrifice in exchange for their use.

The Vault itself was crafted by an enigmatic being known as the **Lamented Artisan**, a Rift-dweller renowned for his macabre creations. Legend says the Artisan designed the box to be *sentient*, binding its fate to its owner. Once attuned, it cannot be opened by anyone else, nor can it be discarded, unless by death or immense magical intervention. It recognizes Priscilla as its master, but it demands a toll for every interaction—a sliver of her vitality—because to open the Vault is to defy the natural order of dimensions.

---

#### **Abilities**
The Vault is much more than a container. Its dimensional nature grants it several unique abilities:  
1. **Infinite Storage**: The box holds far more than its small size suggests, accessing a pocket dimension to store items securely.  
2. **Selective Access**: Only its attuned owner can retrieve items from the Vault, ensuring its contents are never stolen or tampered with.  
3. **Dimensional Resonance**: The box occasionally reveals faint echoes of the Rift it originated from—whispers, visions, or cryptic messages that haunt the attuned owner.  

However, it’s bound by its own twisted logic: each time Priscilla opens the box, it exacts its toll in the form of *physical or emotional pain*. The greater the value of the item stored or retrieved, the harsher the toll. 

---

#### **The Wound**
The Vault’s nature is cruel but deliberate. The pain Priscilla feels upon opening it is not random—it is tied to the Rift’s belief in sacrifice. Over time, this toll leaves subtle scars on her being, not just physically but spiritually. Some scholars speculate that the Vault siphons fragments of her life essence, storing it within itself. What purpose this serves is unknown, but there are whispered theories that her essence may be strengthening the Rift from which the Vault originated.

---

#### **Potential and Mystery**
Priscilla herself may hold the key to uncovering the Vault’s deeper purpose. The artifact seems bound to her fate, as if her connection to it was preordained. Some Rift scholars believe the Vault’s demands could be balanced or even reversed under the right circumstances, while others warn that prolonged use may eventually claim her entirely.

In the wrong hands, the Vault could be used as a weapon—a trove of items too secure for thieves yet devastating for an unprepared owner. For now, it remains Priscilla’s secret and burden.
