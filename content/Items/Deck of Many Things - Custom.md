## Campfire
- D6 times in the next year your party can instantly complete a long rest.

## Door
- Use this card to instantly open a locked door.

## Tomb
- Teleport the party to the nearest necromancers lair.

## Path
- Allows the user to have permanent tracking to a persons location.

## Aberration
- Summon a hostile aberration, if the user can speak same language it is friendly.
