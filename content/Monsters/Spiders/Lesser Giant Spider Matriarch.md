_**Multiattack.**_ The spider makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its lash.

_**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) poison damage. The target must make a DC 11 [Constitution](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Constitution "5e SRD:Constitution") [saving throw](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Saving_Throw "5e SRD:Saving Throw"), taking the poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 [hit points](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Hit_Points "5e SRD:Hit Points"), the target is stable but [poisoned](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Conditions#Poisoned "5e SRD:Conditions") for 1 hour, even after regaining [hit points](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Hit_Points "5e SRD:Hit Points"), and is [paralyzed](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Conditions#Paralyzed "5e SRD:Conditions") while [poisoned](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Conditions#Poisoned "5e SRD:Conditions") in this way.

_**Lash.** Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d10 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

_**Web (Recharge 5-6).** Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ The target is [restrained](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Conditions#Restrained "5e SRD:Conditions") by webbing. As an action, the [restrained](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Conditions#Restrained "5e SRD:Conditions") target can make a DC 12 [Strength](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:Strength "5e SRD:Strength") check, bursting the webbing on a success. The webbing can also be attacked and destroyed ([AC](https://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/5e_SRD:AC "5e SRD:AC") 10; hp 5; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to bludgeoning, poison, and psychic damage).
