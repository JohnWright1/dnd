# Backstory
- Raised in a village in the mountains by parents who were medicine sellers. Parents traveled from time to time to sell medicine to other areas in need. Aukan would stay in a nearby monastery with a friend of the family whilst parents were away. There was a final trip from which parents would never return and nothing would be known of their disappearance.
- The monastery would then raise Aukan and teach their ways to him. The family friend became a mentor. There was a strict training regime to teach discipline and train self defense should it be needed. Although, he often wondered about the disappearance of his parents and found it strange that nothing further had been discovered about them after all these years right into young adulthood.
- Aukan was interested in medicine and healing as per his parents occupation so he planned to continue his parents legacy. It was identified by the family friend that Aukan had a particular aptitude for this and was trained in a rare technique martial technique that allowed for both combat and healing  by manipulating the life force of beings through spirit. 
- On one fateful day, several monks would return from pilgrimage to the monastery. They would start slaying brothers and others in the monastery. They killed many but were fought off by Aukan and others. As they were defeated they let out horrifying cries and wicked spirits were detected leaving their bodies. As the last possessed body fell, an unusual message was spoken in tongue that could not be recognized. Following this the monastery would need to be repaired and the monks would carry on in unison, their will unbroken. 
- Aukan was advised to leave the monastery to go out into the world and explore that lands beyond these mountains, as were several other monks from the monastery following the peculiar events that occurred. Perhaps somewhere out there in far away lands lay the answers to how and why the returning monks were possessed. Aukan was intent on finding answers.
