# Backstory

Priscilla grew up destitute on the streets without a penny to speak of.

Her parents died of a mysterious sickness when she was 14 after being promised treatment, leading to her having to scrape by a living and having a hatred for people that do not keep their word.

She made her living stealing what little she could from passers by, avoiding the authorities and helping/hindering people to whatever advantage she could make.

After years of rough living she happened upon a lock-box with peculiar runes on it, her bloodied hands caused the lock-box to open and bond itself to her through some unknown dark pact. Only she knows what is inside this box.

This has setup a strong feeling that anything placed in this box is only accessible by her, and that it is the only safe place in the world where she can be herself, and store her life savings.

The link between herself and the lock-box is not a healthy one, but she refuses to leave it behind or throw it away as it is her anchor point in the world, perhaps someone can unlock it and free her from this dark bond.

After all, the only truth that she truly knows is that reward can only come from sacrifice.
